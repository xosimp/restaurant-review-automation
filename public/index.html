The real problems

You didn't open a restaurant
to do this.

Most AI vendors sell to chains. Independent restaurants get ignored. These are the four problems I solve — with systems built for operators, not boardrooms.

01
Review & reputation chaos
Hours spent manually responding to Google and Yelp — or worse, not responding at all. Every unanswered review is a lost customer. Cavnar AI connects directly to your Google Business Profile to monitor reviews, suggest AI-drafted replies, and keep your listing accurate — all from one dashboard.
Saves 3–4 hrs/week
02
Labor costs flying blind
Schedules built from memory instead of data. Overstaffed on slow nights, understaffed on busy ones. Overtime slipping through because nobody caught it mid-week. Cavnar AI monitors your labor against your sales in real time — flagging overtime employees before the week ends, surfacing your over- and under-staffed days, and delivering an AI-optimized schedule every week targeting your exact labor goal.
Saves $1,200–2,400/mo
03
Food cost creep nobody catches
Your distributor quietly raises prices 3% on chicken breast. You don't notice until food cost % has drifted from 28% to 34%. Cavnar AI tracks your key ingredient prices week over week, flags every price change with its dollar impact, and monitors which menu items are underwater — so you catch cost creep before it becomes a margin problem.
Recovers $800–2,000/mo
04
Marketing that never happens
No time for consistent social posts, email campaigns, or loyalty follow-ups. Your regulars forget about you. New customers don't find you. Cavnar AI generates and publishes AI-drafted posts directly to Facebook and Instagram from your dashboard — so marketing actually happens, without adding hours to your week.
Saves 6–8 hrs/week
Ranges above are what these problems typically cost a restaurant of 40–120 seats, drawn from industry benchmarks and the operators I've worked with. They are the size of the problem, not a promise about your P&L. Once your data is connected, the dashboard reports what was actually recovered — and shows you the arithmetic behind it.
