Questions

Common questions.

Is there a contract?

Yes. Every plan starts with a short service agreement covering setup, the retainer and cancellation. Setup is paid up front, and the retainer doesn't start until your first 30 days are up. Cancel with 30 days' notice and take your data with you.

What does setup include?

Configuring your modules, connecting your POS and platforms (RPOWER or Toast, Google Business Profile, Instagram and Facebook), an onboarding call, and your restaurant's voice, so every reply and post sounds like you.

Which POS systems work?

RPOWER and Toast connect today. On something else? Say so when you book; anything that can export sales and timecards can be wired up.

Can I add or remove modules later?

Yes. Each added module is $750 one-time setup. Two modules are $649/mo, three $899/mo and all four $1,199/mo, or $6,490, $8,990 and $11,990 a year. Removing one takes effect at the next billing cycle.

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants are live within 3–5 business days. You connect your accounts once; everything else is done for you.

Can I see it before I decide?

Yes. Book a 30-minute demo and see your own restaurant's numbers in Cavnar AI before anything is signed.
