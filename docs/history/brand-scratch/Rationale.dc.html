🧠…the brain & circuits
Restaurant software dresses like daylight: friendly lowercase rounded sans in appetite orange, built to feel harmless at the point of sale. AI tools dress like science fiction: indigo gradients, sparkles, neural nets. Cavnar deliberately refuses both costumes — because it isn’t selling approachability or magic. It’s selling judgment.
THE POSITION — OWN THE NIGHT
Every competitor brands the lunch rush. Cavnar’s real moment is after close — the owner at the bar at midnight, register counted, reading what actually happened today. The product already lives there: black paper, cream ink, a low ember glow. The identity commits to it. Dark, editorial, unhurried — the one brand in the category that looks like the hour when decisions actually get made.
The Seal
A square ring, flat-cut like a branding iron — the oldest mark of ownership in the food business, and the quiet pun the whole identity stands on: Cavnar puts your brand on everything. The ring stays open, and the ember sits in the gap: the one live coal banked overnight, the intelligence that finishes the loop. It reads as a C at 16 pixels and stamps clean into wax, steel, or leather. No fork, no plate, no sparkle.
The Ember Rule
One dot of color, everywhere, and nothing else. Every surface is paper and ink except a single ember — in the seal’s gap, in the V of the wordmark, as the period on a headline. It makes the system instantly recognizable at a glance, impossible to misuse, and cheap in the best way: one spot color on press, one accent token in code. When the ember appears, it always means the same thing — the intelligence is live.
The Wordmark
Custom stencil-cut capitals drawn from raw geometry, not a font anyone can license. One stroke weight, one 15° lean shared by every diagonal, so the A and V lock into a tight constant channel — and the V’s open counter holds the ember. The name literally banks the fire inside itself, which lets “AI” ride as a quiet tag rather than a costume. As the brand matures, the dot alone can carry it.
PALETTE
Paper · Paper 2 · Ink · Ember · Ember 2 — the product’s existing tokens, unchanged. The brand and the app are the same material.
