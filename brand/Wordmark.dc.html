CAVNAR AI — BRAND SYSTEM · 02 WORDMARK
Drawn, not typed
Six letters built from raw geometry — no typeface, no license, nothing a competitor can install. Flat-cut stencil forms, one stroke weight, one lean angle. Strong enough to stand entirely on its own.
CONSTRUCTION
One stroke: 21/100 of cap height. One lean: 15°, shared by every diagonal — so A and V kern into a constant 12-unit channel instead of the dead triangle most straight-sided pairs leave behind. Horizontal cuts land on the same two lines (21, 79) across all six letters.
NEGATIVE SPACE
The counters are the signature: every aperture is the same flat-cut wedge, and the widest one — the V’s open mouth — holds the ember. The name carries its own symbol in its negative space, which is why the wordmark never needs the seal beside it.
REVERSES CLEAN
On paper stock the glow disappears and the ember goes solid — one spot color on press. Nothing about the mark depends on a screen.
HOLDS AT ANY SIZE
Legible to a 13px cap height. Below 60px wide, drop to the seal alone.
